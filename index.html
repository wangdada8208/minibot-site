Minibot 是连接你自己 MiniMax Bot 宿主的移动客户端。模型服务和工具执行由你连接的宿主及其配置的提供商承担。

Minibot connects your phone to your own compatible MiniMax Bot host. Model services and tools are provided by your host and its configured providers.

了解数据使用 / Privacy使用帮助 / Get help

连接自己的宿主
Connect your own host

使用你信任的宿主和连接凭证，查看 Bot 与会话、发送文字并阅读回复。可用功能取决于宿主版本、权限和配置。

Bring a trusted host and connection credentials to browse Bots and conversations, send messages and read replies. Features depend on your host version, permissions and configuration.

先体验离线演示
Try the offline demo

在连接页选择“离线演示 / Try offline demo”。演示使用本机预设回复和不含私人数据的示例，不调用 AI、不执行工具，也不连接真实宿主。退出演示会重置该演示会话。

Choose “Try offline demo” on the connection screen. Sample content and preset replies run locally, with no private sample data, AI calls, tools or real host connection. Exiting resets the demo session.
